Research

PhD, 2024 –

Stochastic optimal control with singular behaviour

Passing from a control problem to its adjoint formulation can produce singular coefficients even when the original problem is well behaved. I study this through an optimal consumption problem with stochastic income,

\[\max_{c}\ \mathbb{E}\left[\int_0^T e^{\delta(T-s)}u_1(c_s)\,\mathrm{d}s + \lambda\,u_2(w_T)\right], \qquad \mathrm{d}w_s = (r_s w_s + \eta_s - c_s)\,\mathrm{d}s.\]

For CRRA utilities the maximum principle gives a coupled FBSDE whose feedback \(c_s \propto P_s^{-1/\gamma_1}\) and terminal condition \(P_T = \lambda w_T^{-\gamma_2}\) are both singular at the boundary, so standard Lipschitz theory does not apply.

Results so far: existence and uniqueness of an optimal consumption process; small-ball estimates for terminal wealth; a verification theorem for the adjoint FBSDE; local well-posedness for short horizons when \(\gamma_2 \le \gamma_1\); and non-existence for long horizons in some regimes with geometric Brownian income. Current work: global well-posedness, DeepBSDE-type numerics, and extensions with investment.

Supervised by Dr Avi Mayorcas and Dr James Foster · SAMBa project page

Research projects

2025

Inverse and implicit function theorems for rough differential equations

Research project, Bath · supervised by Dr Avi Mayorcas

When can the driver of an RDE \(\mathrm{d}Y_t = f(Y_t)\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf{X}_t\) be recovered from its solution? For scalar equations with geometric drivers and nowhere-vanishing \(f\) the recovery is explicit, \(X_t = X_0 + \varphi(Y_t) - \varphi(Y_0)\) with \(\varphi' = 1/f\); when \(f\) has zeros, the solution is confined between them and the recovery becomes ill-conditioned near them.

Integrative Think Tanks and group projects

Jan 2026

Stochastic microscopic model for geographic atrophy

Integrative Think Tank · with the Macular Society

A lattice model for geographic atrophy, the advanced form of dry age-related macular degeneration. Cells of the retinal pigment epithelium accumulate stress from dead neighbours and die once it passes a threshold. A waste-removal term that clears stress faster as the lesion grows reproduces the plateau in lesion size seen clinically, and the final lesion size jumps sharply as the threshold varies.

Spatial corrections for random forests in models of wildfire risk

Group research project · supervised by Dr Evangelos Evangelou

Random forests predict fire probabilities site by site and ignore spatial dependence. We corrected them with an autologistic model, fitting the interaction by pseudo-likelihood and estimating corrected probabilities by Gibbs sampling.
